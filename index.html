A few helpful details

Before you join us

When should I arrive for the wedding?

The wedding muhurtham is at 4:38 AM IST on Saturday, 19 December 2026. Please plan to arrive before that time. If you are travelling overnight, your journey may begin on Friday, 18 December.

Where are the pre-wedding celebrations?

Pelli Koduku and Groom's Haldi are at the Groom's Residence. Pelli Kuthuru and Bride's Haldi are at the Bride's Residence. The reception is at Buddala Convention, Tadepalligudem, on 18 December 2026 from 7:00 PM IST onwards.

How do I save the events?

Choose “Add to calendar” on any event, or “Save all celebrations” to download an .ics file. Open it with your calendar app and confirm the import. These entries include the confirmed start times; end times have not yet been announced.

Which time zone is the schedule in?

Every time shown on this invitation is in India Standard Time (UTC+05:30). The countdown shows the same time remaining wherever you are, and downloaded calendar entries display in your calendar's local time zone.
